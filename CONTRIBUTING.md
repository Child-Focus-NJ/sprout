# Contributing to Project Sprout

While we are a Humanitarian Free and Open Source Software ([HFOSS](https://teachingopensource.org/HFOSS_Education_Overview)) project, we are an active working group of [NYU](https://www.nyu.edu)'s [Tandon School of Engineering](https://engineering.nyu.edu/).

We are only accepting contributors, maintainers, and new members from our school at this time.
